Privacy Notice

Last updated 23 October 2026 · Version 0.1 (draft, pending legal review)

Draft. This page is a first version written in plain language and has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer. It may change.

In short: Futures Radar has no accounts and no analytics today. Your settings and starred markets stay on your device. To show prices, charts, news and fonts, your device connects directly to a few outside services, which can see your device's IP address.

What we collect

Nothing on our own servers. The app has no sign-up, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies.

What stays on your device

The app saves your settings (for example theme, candle size, account size, risk %, max loss, warnings), your starred markets, simulator options, whether you have accepted these pages, and a short log of app errors in your browser's local storage on your device. It is not sent to us. You can remove it by clearing this site's data in your browser. The error log is only shared if you copy it yourself (Settings → App health).

Services your device connects to

To work, the app asks these services for data directly from your device. Like any website, they can see your device's IP address and basic browser details, and they handle that under their own privacy policies:

  • Binance, Gate and Bybit public market data (market lists, prices, candles, live price streams).
  • GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com and GitHub Pages), which hosts the app and the news file.
  • Google Fonts, for the Inter font.
  • jsDelivr, for the chart library.
  • A relay address, only if you enter one yourself in Settings.

Links you open (for example a news article or X) take you to those sites, which have their own policies.

Imported files

If the app lets you load a file (for example a trade history export), it is read on your device and not uploaded.

Children

The app is not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

If accounts, analytics or anything else that handles personal data is added later, this notice will be updated first and the date and version at the top will change.

1. What the service is

Futures Radar ("the app") is a free web app that lists crypto futures markets and describes what their price charts show, using common chart indicators (for example Bollinger bands, Supertrend, RSI and MACD). It also offers a simulator that replays past prices, a news page and calculators. Every user sees the same readings for the same market and candle size. The app does not know your positions, goals or finances and does not tailor anything to you.

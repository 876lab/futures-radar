## 2026-10-25 (owner request: Fibonacci levels)
- **Fibonacci levels on the chart screen** (information only; signal rules, scores and entry/stop/target maths unchanged). New pure functions `zigzag`, `fibLevels`, `fibZone` (after `recommend`). Swings come from a zigzag that needs a move of at least 3× ATR(14) against the last swing, over the last 200 candles. The measured move is the last zigzag leg, except while price is pulling back by less than that leg (then the leg before it is kept, so the pullback is measured against the main move). Levels: 0%, 23.6%, 38.2%, 50%, 61.8%, 78.6%, 100% and extensions 127.2% / 161.8%. A "breakout" is a still-running move that has gone past the previous swing on its side.
- Chart: dotted price lines (grey retracements, blue extensions; solid 0% / 100%), on by default with a new **Fibonacci** chip (`state.ind.fib`; older saved chip settings get it switched on). Card under the chart: the move measured, how much has been given back and what that zone usually means, breakout wording, next level above and below with distance, all levels in a fold-out, and a note that the evidence is mixed. Help entry added.
- **Nightly backtest** now records the Fibonacci zone at each simulated entry (only candles up to the signal) and reports "Where price was on the last move (Fibonacci)" in Simulator → Last night's backtest, so the zones get judged on real Gate data instead of trusted.
- Tests: 12 engine tests (swing detection, measured move, 61.8% and 161.8% prices, zone bands, breakout, next levels, no look-ahead); browser test checks the card in both themes and, where the chart library loads, the lines and the chip.
- Sources: Shanaev & Gibson (2022), "Can returns breed like rabbits? Econometric tests for Fibonacci retracements" (summary: https://paperswithbacktest.com/strategies/can-returns-breed-like-rabbits-econometric-tests-for-fibonacci-retracements) found 38.2/50/61.8% levels predictive for stock indices and FX, and that adding 23.6/76.4/78.6% levels weakened the model; "Automatic identification and evaluation of Fibonacci retracements: empirical evidence from three equity markets", Expert Systems with Applications (2021) (https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0957417421012495); "A computational exploration of the efficacy of Fibonacci sequences in technical analysis and trading" (2006) (http://aeconf.com/articles/may2006/aef070109.pdf), more sceptical; Presto Labs on a 61.8% signal for Binance perpetuals, with no comparison against random levels (https://www.prestolabs.io/research/technical-analysis-in-statistical-arbitrage-i-fibonacci-retracement); level conventions (23.6–78.6% retracements, 127.2/161.8% extensions, swing selection) from altFINS (https://altfins.com/knowledge-base/what-are-fibonacci-retracement-levels/). No crypto study found that tests Fibonacci levels against random levels, hence the backtest buckets.

## 2026-10-24 (owner decision: simpler confirmation rule, option a)
- **"Confirmed" now means:** at least one of the 4H and 1D trends agrees with the setup, and no delisting is scheduled or announced. Chosen by the owner from the first nightly backtest (3,174 Gate trades, Dec 2025 – Oct 2026): the old 5-check rule did not help (confirmed 37% to target 1, −0.07R vs not confirmed 39%, 0.00R), while setups where neither 4H nor 1D agreed did worst (33%, −0.16R vs 39–41% otherwise).

The box header says why a setup isn't confirmed (neither trend agrees, or delisting). Setups (switch still on by default), its footnote, Settings and Help reworded.

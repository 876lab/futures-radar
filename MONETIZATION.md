# Futures Radar: monetization plan

Agreed with the owner on 22 October 2026. Strategy in one line: **prove the signals in public first, grow a small group of real users, then earn from referral links, and only charge once results and legal advice support it.**

## Where the value is

- The niche: beginner-to-intermediate crypto futures traders who trade on their phones (Gate, Binance) and want plain-language direction, entry, stop, targets and position size. TradingView is the main alternative and is complex; its paid plans run $14.95–$69.95/month (monthly billing, Oct 2026).
- Today the app has no users, no revenue and unproven signals, and its code is public, so its resale value is low. Value comes from a **public track record** and **active users**.

## Who to target

1. Beginner and intermediate futures traders on phones, especially Gate and Binance users. Start with Jamaica and the Caribbean (word of mouth, WhatsApp and Telegram groups), then other English-speaking markets where retail futures trading is common and price matters.
2. Later: trading educators and signal communities who want a tool for their members.

## Ways to earn (in the order we'll try them)

1. **Exchange referral links**: "Trade this on Gate" with the owner's referral code, clearly disclosed. Keeps the app free; lowest legal risk. Needs users first.
2. **Free + paid tier (~$9–12/month)**: free screener, Hot and News; paid Setups signals, Simulator, alerts, trade-history analysis and risk tools. 100 subscribers ≈ $1,000/month; 500 ≈ $5,000/month. Needs accounts and payments (Lemon Squeezy pays out to Jamaican bank accounts and handles sales tax; USDT is another option).
3. **App + community or course**: Telegram/Discord or a beginner course with the app included.
4. Not planned: ads.

## Must-dos before charging anyone

- **Prove the signals.** Track every live signal publicly for 60–90 days, losses included. Never market profits the record doesn't show.
- **Legal advice.** The app generates its own "Enter long/short" calls. In the US, paid advice on futures can require registration as a Commodity Trading Advisor (CFTC); other countries have their own rules (Jamaica: Financial Services Commission). Get a lawyer's view before charging, and frame the app as education and tools.
- **Protect the code.** The GitHub repository and site are public today. A paid version needs real accounts with paid features served from a server, not from the public page.
- **Check exchange terms** for commercial use of public market data and for referral programs.

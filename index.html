How to read Futures Radar

Each row is one market. When the same coin trades on several exchanges, it appears once. Its price and 24-hour change come from the exchange with the most trading, and volume adds up all of them.

Getting around

Bottom bar
Markets, Setups, Simulator, News and Starred. On a computer the same buttons sit above the list.
Compact
Shows one line per market so more fit on screen.
Swipes on a chart
Swipe in from the left edge, or use your phone's back gesture, to return to the list. Swipe left or right on the signal card to change candle size.
Small line on each row
The price over the last 24 hours, green if it ended higher.

The signal

Enter long
The trend is up, the bigger timeframe agrees, and there's a fresh buy trigger without price being stretched. Buy in the entry zone and set the stop.
Enter short
The same, pointing down. You profit if price falls.
Hold
A trend is in place but this isn't a good spot to open a new trade, usually because price has already run. If you're in, stay in. If not, wait for the "better entry" price.
Avoid
No clear trend, or the short and bigger timeframes disagree. Sitting out is a position too.
Stop loss
Where the idea is wrong. Close the trade there, no exceptions.
Targets
Target 1 is 1.5 times your risk, target 2 is 3 times. Many traders take half at target 1 and move the stop to entry.
Confidence
How many signals line up, from 0 to 100. Above 70 is strong agreement.
B and S on the chart
Where Supertrend flipped to buy (B) or sell (S).

The indicators

Supertrend (10, 3)
A trailing line under price in an uptrend (green) or above it in a downtrend (red). A close through it flips the direction.
Bollinger bands (20, 2)
A channel around the 20-candle average. Price near the top band is stretched, near the middle is a typical pullback, near the bottom is weak.
RSI 6 / 12 / 24
How hard price has been pushed over short, medium and longer spans. Above 70 is often overheated, below 30 oversold.
MACD (DIF, DEA)
Momentum. When DIF crosses above DEA and bars turn green, buyers are gaining. The reverse favours sellers.
EMA 20 / 50 / 200
Moving averages. Price above them, with the short line on top, is a classic uptrend.
Funding
A small fee swapped between longs and shorts every few hours on perpetuals. Positive means longs pay shorts.
Volume
Dollar value traded. A move on rising volume is more trustworthy.
News
Headlines from crypto and financial news sites, the Federal Reserve and the SEC, refreshed every 15 minutes. Fear & Greed sums up market mood from 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed).
Live
The dot at the top shows how fresh prices are. "Live · now" means ticks are streaming in. "Delayed" means no tick for 30 seconds; the app reconnects on its own.

Settings

Appearance

The most you will let one trade lose if it hits the stop. A good start is about twice your average losing trade (Gate shows this in your position history). Size this trade then warns in red when a plan would lose more, and shows a smaller size that fits. Leave empty to switch off.

Only needed if an exchange refuses requests from your browser. Put {url} where the exchange address goes; without it the address is added to the end.

Data feeds

Market lists come from each exchange's public REST API. Prices and the open chart then stream live over each exchange's WebSocket feed: Binance USDⓈ-M futures, Gate USDT perpetuals, and Bybit USDT perpetuals and futures. Starred markets and sizing inputs stay on this device.
